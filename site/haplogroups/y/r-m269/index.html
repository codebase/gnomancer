Haplogroup R-M269

R›R-M343›R-M269

The main Western European branch, carried by Bronze Age migrations; R-U106 is typical of Germanic regions, R-P312 and R-L21 of Atlantic Europe, R-U152 of the Alpine region and Italy.

How gnomancer finds it

The Y haplogroup is called from the Y-chromosome sites on your chip by yhaplo (ISOGG tree) with a second opinion from Yleaf on the current YFull, FamilyTreeDNA and ISOGG trees. Arrays type a few thousand Y sites, so the call stops at roughly this depth; Y sequencing (e.g. Big Y-700) is needed for a terminal subclade.

Run it on your own raw file: install gnomancer (nothing is uploaded), or ask your AI assistant with the MCP server.
